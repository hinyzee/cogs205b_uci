Implement the `BayesFactor` class in `bayes_factor.py`

Class structure:
- BayesFactor(n, k) — n is total trials, k is observed successes.
- Do NOT change the constructor signature. Do NOT add required arguments.

Methods to implement:
- likelihood(self, theta) — binomial likelihood: C(n, k) · θ^k · (1−θ)^(n−k).
- evidence_slab(self) — marginal likelihood under slab prior θ ~ U(0, 1).
- evidence_spike(self) — marginal likelihood under spike prior θ ~ U(a, b)
  with a = 0.47, b = 0.53.
- bayes_factor(self) — ratio evidence_spike() / evidence_slab().

Priors to use:
- Slab: θ ~ Uniform(0, 1), density 1.
- Spike: θ ~ Uniform(a, b), density 1/(b−a), with a and b as above.
